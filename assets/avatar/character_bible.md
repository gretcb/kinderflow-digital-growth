# Kinder Signs Avatar — Character Bible

## Character identity

A warm, friendly Spanish woman in her early 30s.

She has:
- dark brown eyes
- dark brown hair
- a casual high messy bun
- a few loose face-framing strands
- natural makeup
- a warm, approachable expression
- a realistic adult appearance

## Personality

She is calm, reassuring, friendly and educational.

Her presence should feel natural and trustworthy rather than corporate, theatrical or overly animated.

She maintains comfortable eye contact with the viewer and communicates with patience and clarity.

## Movement style

Her movements are:
- slow
- smooth
- deliberate
- easy to follow

Hand and finger movements must remain clearly visible.

Movement precision takes priority over decorative gestures or dramatic acting.

## Visual direction

The character should remain recognisable and visually consistent across Kinder Signs content.

Preferred presentation:
- photorealistic
- natural skin texture
- soft natural lighting
- upper-body or mid-shot framing
- uncluttered environment
- both hands visible whenever the sign requires them

## Clothing

Use simple, comfortable clothing suitable for an early-childhood educational setting.

Preferred:
- plain knitwear
- simple cotton tops
- soft neutral or pastel tones

Avoid:
- busy patterns
- large logos
- reflective accessories
- clothing that reduces hand or arm visibility

Clothing may change with the routine or context while preserving the same character identity.

## Voice direction

When voice is used:
- Spanish
- warm and gentle
- natural rather than announcer-like
- calm pace
- clearly articulated
- encouraging
- suitable for family guidance

Avoid robotic, promotional or overly energetic delivery.

## Core rule

The character defines the look.
The reference movement defines the sign.
