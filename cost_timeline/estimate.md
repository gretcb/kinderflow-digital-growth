# KinderFlow Cost and Timeline Estimate

Document status: final capstone reconciliation
Estimate type: incremental validation estimate
Total elapsed period: 8 to 9 weeks
Controlled service test: 3 to 4 weeks within the total period
Pilot scope: 2 to 3 nursery schools and 3 to 5 signs
Currency: EUR

## Executive estimate

The incremental cost to prepare and run the controlled pilot is estimated at EUR 5,500 to EUR 17,300.

The estimate has two parts:

- pilot readiness: EUR 1,700 to EUR 5,300;
- controlled pilot and service work: EUR 3,800 to EUR 12,000.

The upper bound is EUR 17,300 because the detailed high estimates sum to EUR 17,300. EUR 17,800 is not supported by the line items.

This is a project estimate, not a supplier quote, purchase approval, production budget, or forecast. Work begins from an existing local Computer Vision technical feasibility artifact and local nursery workflow. Current prototype effort is sunk, unpriced, and excluded.

## Scope boundary

### Included

- refine the existing local technical evidence path for the 3 to 5 pilot signs;
- refine the nursery review and assignment workflow;
- adapt and harden the implemented local/session assignment-driven family mini-library for consented controlled use;
- configure minimal evaluation and trace evidence;
- clear source, rights, provenance, and review status for every pilot sign;
- recruit and coordinate 2 to 3 nursery schools;
- run a 3 to 4 week controlled service test;
- conduct educator, family, reviewer, and economic-buyer research;
- produce a measured GO, ITERATE, or STOP decision.

### Excluded

- value of work already completed on the current prototype;
- public production launch;
- full multi-tenant production architecture;
- production authentication, durable accounts, billing, support desk, and broad integrations;
- production hosting, security assurance, identity operations, and monitoring;
- external legal or privacy advice unless explicitly procured;
- a broad production sign library;
- child video, child scoring, and automated educational decisions;
- taxes, financing, travel not included in coordination, and contingency outside the stated ranges.

Production build remains TBD and must be estimated after the controlled pilot. If external legal or privacy review is required, it must be scoped and the estimate rebaselined before purchase.

## Current baseline

The estimate does not assume a blank-sheet build.

Current evidence includes:

- a local Computer Vision technical feasibility artifact with traceable artefacts and explicit status;
- deterministic HUMAN mode and optional provider-backed paths;
- a local professional record and nursery assignment flow;
- a session-based assignment-driven Family Experience and mini-library using synthetic groups and fictional children;
- content review-state and provenance controls;
- a local validation and evidence workflow.

Current limitations include:

- no production accounts or durable multi-site assignments;
- no real family-delivery service;
- no real family identities or accounts, authentication or authorisation, durable cross-session or cross-device persistence, notifications, tenant isolation, production correction or deletion workflows, or external nursery-platform integrations;
- no measured pilot reliability, family use, willingness to pay, reviewer cost, or support burden;
- incomplete sign-specific rights and presentation clearance;
- production infrastructure and operating cost not scoped.

The cost below is for refinement, clearance, controlled delivery, research, and decision evidence.

## Timeline

The work is planned as an 8 to 9 week elapsed programme. Some tasks overlap. The controlled service test occupies 3 to 4 weeks within that programme.

### Week 1: decision setup

Outcomes:

- confirm 2 to 3 nursery participants and named school sponsors;
- freeze pilot scope at 3 to 5 signs;
- confirm economic-buyer interviews and price tests;
- approve data minimisation, consent or notice, retention, deletion, and incident routes;
- appoint technical, product, privacy, content-review, research, and pilot owners;
- record KPI baselines and sign-off thresholds.

Gate:

- no sign processing or participant-data activity begins until ownership, privacy, rights, and measurement responsibilities are accepted.

### Weeks 1 to 3: readiness refinement

Outcomes:

- freeze technical versions and rehearse fallback;
- refine nursery assignment;
- configure the implemented assignment-driven family mini-library for consented, identity-backed pilot access;
- configure evaluation evidence;
- prepare educator and family task scripts;
- establish manual support and correction routes.

Gate:

- every planned workflow has an observable event or manual evidence record;
- no prohibited data enters model or trace paths.

### Weeks 2 to 4: content clearance and pilot rehearsal

Outcomes:

- select the final 3 to 5 signs;
- complete source, rights, provenance, review, and family-presentation checks;
- record initial reviewer time and rework;
- rehearse assignment, delivery, withdrawal, failure, and incident scenarios;
- train the limited pilot staff.

Gate:

- provenance complete for 100% of signs;
- review exceptions documented for 100%;
- unreviewed or blocked content cannot be delivered;
- critical rehearsal failures are closed or the pilot stops.

### Weeks 4 to 8: controlled service test

Duration:

- 3 to 4 weeks, depending on school scheduling and readiness.

Outcomes:

- activate all 3 Little Steps groups;
- observe first assignment and completion without help;
- collect at least 2 repeat assignments per group;
- deliver reviewed family items through the mini-library;
- record family access, repeat use, clarity, usefulness, questions, and withdrawal;
- measure technical success, reviewer effort, support burden, and exceptions;
- conduct named-buyer price and continuation interviews.

Cadence:

- weekly safety and boundary check;
- weekly workflow and support review;
- weekly evidence-quality review;
- immediate escalation for content, privacy, rights, or safeguarding incidents.

### Weeks 8 to 9: analysis and decision

Outcomes:

- reconcile event and manual evidence;
- update the nursery value case;
- update review, reuse, support, CAC, and provider-cost assumptions;
- update Low, Base, and High economics;
- record limitations and missing evidence;
- issue a GO, ITERATE, or STOP decision.

Gate:

- no broader launch decision is made from anecdotal interest or incomplete safety evidence.

## Critical path

The critical path is:

1. nursery and owner participation confirmed;
2. rights and reviewer availability confirmed;
3. privacy and family-delivery rules approved;
4. 3 to 5 signs cleared;
5. assignment-driven family mini-library ready;
6. event and manual evidence capture ready;
7. controlled service test complete;
8. price-specific continuation evidence collected;
9. decision review complete.

The schedule slips if any of the first six items is incomplete. Scope should be reduced before safety, rights, or evidence quality is compressed.

## Estimate controls

### Change control

Re-estimate before accepting:

- more than 3 pilot schools;
- more than 5 signs;
- public production access;
- child, family, or staff personal-data integrations beyond the approved minimum;
- direct messaging or broad notifications;
- new production identity or billing systems;
- unplanned travel or external professional services;
- material new accessibility, translation, or language scope.

### Spend controls

- approve a specific figure within each range before work begins;
- link invoices or time records to the relevant workstream;
- record actual effort for technical refinement, workflow, evaluation, review, research, production, and coordination separately;
- do not move unused safety, rights, or review budget to feature expansion without decision-owner approval;
- rebaseline the programme if an excluded legal, privacy, hosting, or production item becomes mandatory.

### Evidence controls

- label all actuals, estimates, and scenarios;
- report variance from plan;
- distinguish a completed task from a validated outcome;
- keep the 8 to 9 week elapsed timeline separate from the 3 to 4 week controlled test;
- keep pilot school count separate from ROI average paying-centre scenarios.

## Relationship to ongoing operations and recurring economics

The ROI model uses the following annual scenario allowances after validation:

- annual fixed operating cost: EUR 2,400, EUR 4,800, or EUR 6,000;
- variable cost per active centre-year: EUR 180, EUR 240, or EUR 300;
- annual reusable-content production: EUR 3,000, EUR 4,000, or EUR 6,000.

These are calculated-scenario inputs, not quotes or observed operating costs. They must be updated from pilot evidence.

The pilot must therefore capture:

- provider and API use;
- manual processing and recovery;
- reviewer time and rework;
- support and onboarding;
- content creation;
- coordination;
- selling effort by prospect.

Production build is not included in those recurring allowances.

## Decision rules

### GO

Proceed to a separately scoped next phase when:

- all hard safety, privacy, rights, and review gates hold;
- exact educator targets are met;
- signed family thresholds are met;
- at least 2 schools state credible continuation intent at a tested price;
- measured reviewer and support burden can update a viable economic case;
- production build and operating costs are scoped before approval.

### ITERATE

Run one bounded correction cycle when:

- controls hold and user value is evident;
- a workflow, family-use, price, review-burden, or reliability target misses;
- the correction has a named owner, added cost, and decision date.

Any iteration cost outside the approved range requires rebaselining.

### STOP

Stop the expansion when:

- rights, review, privacy, or safeguarding controls cannot be maintained;
- the workflow remains unusable after bounded correction;
- no participating school identifies a credible paid path;
- production or content economics remain incompatible with tested pricing.

## Final estimate statement

The appropriate planning envelope is EUR 5,500 to EUR 17,300 over 8 to 9 weeks, including a 3 to 4 week controlled service test with 2 to 3 nursery schools and 3 to 5 signs. This buys evidence for a decision. It does not buy production readiness, a broad content library, or a guaranteed commercial return.
