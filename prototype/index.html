When to use it

Use it during snack time or playtime when your child wants more of something.

How to model it

Say the word while showing the sign. Repeat naturally in the routine.

School-home connection

The school uses this sign in the same routine this week, creating one clear cue across school and home.

Boundaries

This is routine guidance, not clinical advice. It does not promise faster development. Do not force repetition.

Try it in context: Offer a little more of an activity or snack, say “more”, and model the studied sign naturally. Follow the child’s response without requiring them to copy it.

Internal operations

What KinderFlow manages internally

KinderFlow prepares and checks each sign card. The school only chooses or receives approved content.

  • Movement quality check
  • Family card drafting
  • Content checks
  • Expert review
  • Library updates

Movement checks support quality review; they do not confirm final sign correctness. Expert review is required before a sign card is released.
