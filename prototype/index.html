When to use it

Use it during snack time or playtime when your child wants more of something.

How to model it

Say the word while showing the sign. Repeat naturally in the routine.

School-home connection

The same sign is used at school this week, so the child sees a consistent cue.

Boundaries

This is family guidance, not clinical advice. Do not force repetition.

Try it in context: Offer a little more of an activity or snack, say “more”, and model the studied sign naturally. Follow the child’s response without requiring them to copy it.

Internal operations

What KinderFlow manages internally

This backend supports the Kinder Signs library. The school does not manage the AI workflow.

  • Computer Vision motion capture
  • LLM-assisted content drafting
  • Quality checks
  • Human review
  • Library versioning

Computer Vision supports motion representation, not final sign correctness. The LLM supports content drafting, not sign validation.
