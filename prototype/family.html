Short sign video

When to use it

Use it during snack time or playtime when your child wants more of something.

How to model it

Say the word while showing the sign. Repeat it naturally in the routine.

School-home connection

The group uses the same sign during the same routine, creating one familiar cue.

Boundaries

This is routine guidance, not clinical advice. It does not promise faster development. Do not force repetition.

Try it in context: Offer a little more of an activity or snack, say “more”, and model the sign naturally. Follow the child’s response without requiring them to copy it.
