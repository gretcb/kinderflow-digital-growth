Deterministic checks

Required sign, language, length, fields, requested context and blocked/licensed keywords.

Evaluator checks

Instruction adherence, contextual relevance, tone alignment and age-appropriate simplicity.

LangSmith observability

Prompt/output visibility, evaluation evidence, debugging and monitoring for the LLM content step.

LangSmith does not validate sign biomechanics, MediaPipe output or linguistic sign correctness.
