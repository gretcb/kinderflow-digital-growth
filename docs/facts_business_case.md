## Evidence labels

- Founder-observed: supplied from direct operating knowledge and not independently audited.
- Public evidence: limited to the fact supported by a cited public source.
- Calculated scenario: arithmetic from stated inputs; not an observed result or forecast.
- Benchmark: an external reference point; not proof of KinderFlow demand.
- Project estimate: a planning allowance; not a quote or invoice.
- Pilot hypothesis: an assumption or target that requires testing.
- TBD: unknown and excluded until measured.

## Reuse, CAC, and LTV

Content allocation formula:

> Allocated content cost per school = (creation + API + review + rework) / schools using the approved asset

At an illustrative EUR 1,000 fully loaded approved-asset cost:

- 1 school: EUR 1,000;
- 3 schools: approximately EUR 333 each;
- 10 schools: EUR 100 each;
- 30 schools: approximately EUR 33 each.

The EUR 1,000 value is a pilot hypothesis. The pilot must measure every component, and reuse has not yet been realised.

CAC formula:

> CAC = (sales labour + demonstrations + travel + onboarding) / new paying centres

LTV formula:

> LTV = annual contribution per centre x expected retained years - incremental support cost

CAC, retention, and LTV remain not calculable from observed KinderFlow customers.
