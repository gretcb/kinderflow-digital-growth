## Evidence rules

- An importable n8n export alone is not a live workflow run; KinderFlow separately preserves a successful historical runtime screenshot.

- A separately prepared Gemini video is not current landmark-generated output.
- A screen prototype is not production delivery.
- A local, session-based assignment-driven family mini-library is not authenticated, durable production family delivery.
- A successful schema or hash check is not publication approval.

## Three priority risks

### 1. Sign fidelity and review feasibility

Client fact: zero production signs are published.

Action: complete rights, sign, visual, and publication review for the pilot set.

Target: three to five signs with complete evidence and no critical content incident.

Owner: Content Operations and a qualified sign reviewer.

Decision rule: stop if reviewers cannot approve the set reliably or at a workable cost.

### 2. Willingness to pay and repeated adoption

Client fact: adjacent family and educator offers are paid, but KinderFlow has zero paying centres and no retention evidence.

Action: test EUR 600, EUR 1,200, and EUR 1,800 with named budget owners; record first use, repeated assignments, support burden, and a price-specific continuation decision.

Target: at least two participating schools identify a budget owner and state credible continuation intent at a tested price, alongside the educator repeat-use target.

Owner: Product Owner, commercial lead, and nursery sponsors.

Decision rule: iterate when users see value but price, packaging, or buyer process remains fixable; stop when no participating school identifies a viable recurring paid path.

### 3. Rights and provenance

Client fact: Open Peeps provenance is recorded, but several reference and Gemini usage rights remain open.

Action: document source authority, display, adaptation, distribution, expiry, and withdrawal for each pilot asset.

Target: 100 percent pilot-sign provenance, including rights and review.

Owner: Product Owner and rights owner.

Decision rule: block every asset with an unknown external-use status.

## Material gate outside the top three: secure family delivery

Client fact: Family View is an assignment-driven local/session mini-library, not an authenticated, durable or tenant-separated family account service.

Action: build and test group-first, role-based, nursery-separated family access.

Target: zero cross-family or cross-nursery access and zero personal-data model transfers.

Owner: Engineering, privacy owner, and nursery controller.

Decision rule: stop the pilot on any wrong-recipient access or unauthorised AI transfer.

## Hard pilot boundaries

- child video processed in core scope equals zero;
- child scoring equals zero;
- automated educational decisions equal zero;
- unreviewed content delivered equals zero;
- blocked content delivered equals zero;
- personal child or family data sent to LLM or LangSmith equals zero;
- pilot sign provenance, including rights and review, equals 100 percent; and
- review exceptions with rationale equals 100 percent.
